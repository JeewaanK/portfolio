Investment Philosophy

Full Philosophy

The starting question is the same whether it's a stock or a loan: what specific number is the market implying, and what does the data actually say?

On NuScale, a reverse DCF extracted a 45.2% market-implied pipeline conversion probability against a 65% stage-gate estimate. On CoreWeave, the question was whether GPU collateral would hold its value long enough — an expert call on useful life answered it. In both cases, observable data was ahead of the consensus view.

Read the full framework

Process

01
Extract the market-implied number
Reverse DCF or spread decomposition to find the specific assumption being priced. Not a narrative — a number.
02
Find primary data that disputes it
Stage-gate close rates, foot traffic, collateral life, credit metrics. Something observable that leads the consensus model.
03
Build a falsifiable case
State what would have to be true for the thesis to be wrong. If there's no clear answer, it isn't specific enough.
