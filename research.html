CRWV
CoreWeave $5.6B First-Lien Delayed-Draw Term Loan · SOFR + 600 · Feb 2026

Recommended a buy on the term loan for the Oaktree Case Competition. Projected leverage and free cash flow through 2030 against a ~$60B debt-funded buildout, and benchmarked the loan against Oracle, Iridium and Viasat loans and the leveraged loan index. Structural protections — take-or-pay contracts, tiered LTV advance rates (90% IG / 60% non-IG counterparties) and an SPV cash trap — carry the downside case. An expert call with Wolfe Research on GPU useful life underpins 40–60% recovery on the collateral. Debriefed with Oaktree's Global Opportunities team.

Honors Thesis · Econometrics

Applied Econometrics · R ECON 580 Honors · Spring 2025
The Impact of Socioeconomic Status on Maternal Healthcare Utilization in Nepal

Survey-weighted logit and OLS models on the 2016 Nepal Demographic and Health Survey (~5,060 mothers), with PSU-clustered standard errors, province fixed effects and wealth × education interactions. Women in the richest quintile have 5–6× the odds of a facility birth versus the poorest, and wealth and education compound rather than add. Postnatal care shows almost no socioeconomic gradient at all.
